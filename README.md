🚖 **Maximizing Taxi Cab Revenue through Payment Type Analysis** 🚖  

In the competitive world of taxi services, increasing revenue is key to ensuring driver satisfaction and business growth. This project explores the link between payment methods and fare amounts, leveraging data-driven insights to uncover opportunities for boosting taxi drivers' earnings.  

**Objective:**  
Conduct an A/B test using Python for hypothesis testing and descriptive statistics to analyze the relationship between fare amounts and payment types. The goal is to determine whether credit card payments generate higher fares compared to cash payments and how this insight can be used to encourage revenue-maximizing behaviors.  

**Key Research Question:**  
- Does payment type significantly impact total fare amounts?  
- How can taxi drivers encourage customers to adopt more profitable payment methods without compromising their experience?  

**Insights Uncovered:**  
Dive into the economics of taxi services to discover actionable strategies for optimizing payment methods, increasing revenue, and enhancing the overall customer experience.
